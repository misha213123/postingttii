PostingTTII Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

PostingTTII is designed as a locally run desktop publishing tool.

Information processed

When you connect a supported social media account, PostingTTII processes OAuth access and refresh tokens, basic account identifiers or display names returned by the platform, and the video files and captions you choose to publish.

Local storage

Connected-account tokens are stored locally on the user's computer in encrypted form. Social media account passwords are not requested or stored by PostingTTII.

Third-party services

When you request a generated caption, the text context you provide may be sent to OpenAI. When you publish content, the selected video and caption are sent to the applicable social media platform through its official API.

Data control

Users can disconnect connected accounts from the application. Local application data can also be removed from the computer running PostingTTII.

Purpose limitation

PostingTTII uses connected account data only to identify authorized accounts, maintain authorization, and perform user-requested publishing actions.
